## Full description (draft)

> Papercup turns your phone into a voice and chat remote for your **own** Papercup
> desktop. Pair once by scanning the code on your desktop, then talk to it hands-free
> or type — your requests go straight to the machine you control, not to a cloud
> middleman.
>
> - **Talk to your desktop** — natural voice commands, spoken replies.
> - **Private by design** — your voice and messages go to your own desktop; no ads,
>   no behavioral tracking, no data sale.
> - **Pair in seconds** — scan the QR code your desktop shows.
> - **Stay in the loop** — get notified when your desktop needs you.
>
> Papercup requires a paired Papercup desktop to work.
